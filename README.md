# supply-chain-dashboard
Power BI -samsung Supply Chain &amp; Logistics Dashboard
Overview
This project is a Supply Chain Dashboard built in Power BI to understand the complete business flow from Supplier to Customer.
The goal was to convert raw data into meaningful insights and identify where inefficiencies exist in the system.

Dashboard Flow

Supplier → Inventory → Shipment → Customer

Each section focuses on a different part of the supply chain to give a complete picture. 

Key Metrics
Revenue & Profit
Shipment Quantity & Delivery %
Inventory & Safety Stock
Supplier Lead Time & Order Quantity
Defective Units

Key Analysis
Shipment
Total shipments and delivery performance tracked
Delay analysis based on carriers
Monthly shipping cost trends
Supplier
Supplier performance based on lead time
Order quantity contribution
Unit cost comparison
Inventory & Production
Inventory levels and safety stock
Defective units trend
Product-wise defect analysis
Overview
Complete business summary with revenue, profit, and margin
Quick insights into overall performance   

Key Learnings
Delays in shipment are mostly influenced by carrier and operational constraints
Inventory imbalance can directly affect order fulfillment
Supplier performance plays a critical role in overall efficiency

Tools Used
Power BI
Data Modeling
Data Visualization
